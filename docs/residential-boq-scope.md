# Residential BOQ scope

Initial work breakdown structure for BuildCost PH residential estimates.

1. General requirements and temporary facilities
2. Site preparation and earthworks
3. Concrete and foundation works
4. Reinforcing steel and formworks
5. Masonry and plastering
6. Roofing and waterproofing
7. Doors, windows and glazing
8. Ceiling and architectural finishes
9. Floor and wall finishes
10. Painting and coatings
11. Plumbing and sanitary works
12. Electrical works
13. External works and drainage
14. Labor, equipment, overhead and contingency

For each BOQ item, record description, unit, quantity, unit rate, amount, scope notes and source/date for pricing. Keep material, labor and equipment components separately identifiable when developing detailed unit-price analysis.

All quantities must be reviewed against the approved drawings and specifications. This scope is a starter checklist, not a complete project specification.
